Our Story

How it started

Once upon a time

Hannah’s side

Liam and I had our first date on March 14, 2020. The plan was to make Shepherd’s pie together, to honor Pi Day and get a head start on St. Patrick’s Day. But the world was also ending, and every grocery store was completely out of meat. I was not about to make chicken-feet Shepherd’s pie.

When Liam showed up, I broke the bad news, and he didn’t care one bit. He rummaged through whatever we had lying around and cheffed up the best mac and cheese I’ve ever eaten in my entire life. That’s when I knew I really liked him: Liam can make the most out of any situation, and BONUS: he’s an amazing chef!

Later that night, trying to impress him, I dug out some leftover Fourth of July fireworks and lit a full-on mortar in the backyard of our HOA neighborhood. In the middle of the night. In March. The phone started ringing with neighbors asking if it was us, or if they should call the cops because it sounded like a gunshot. Of course they assumed it was the Lyons doing something crazy.

I’m not sure I impressed him the same way he impressed me with his cooking, but he’s still here years later, so I think it worked. :)
