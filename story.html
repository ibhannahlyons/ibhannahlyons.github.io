The question

The proposal

Liam planned to propose in the snowy mountains of Park City, Utah. Unfortunately, on the very first day of vacation, he exploded his knee on a double black diamond, coming to a sudden stop only by wrapping his leg around a tree. The damage: a fully torn ACL, a partially torn meniscus, a sprained MCL, a fractured tibia, and something going on with his kneecap. (He’s fully recovered now; we just want everyone to understand the gravity of the situation!)

Liam was not deterred. With help from Hannah’s cousins and her mom and dad, he made sure she’d be in the right place at the right time. On March 5, 2026, he waited for the snow to fall, then walked her out to the bridge they crossed every morning to get to the slopes.

There, Liam got down on one knee (even with his exploded knee!) and asked Hannah, “Will you marry me?”

She said yes!
