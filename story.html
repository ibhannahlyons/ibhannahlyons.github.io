Our Story

How it started

Once upon a time

Liam and Hannah had their first date on March 14, 2020. The plan was to make Shepherd’s pie together, to honor Pi Day and get a head start on St. Patrick’s Day. But the world was also ending, and every grocery store was completely out of meat. Hannah did not want to make chicken-feet Shepherd’s pie.

When Liam arrived, Hannah broke the bad news, but Liam was too busy noticing how beautiful she looked to care one bit. He rummaged through whatever Hannah had lying around and cheffed up the best mac and cheese she’d ever eaten in her entire life. That’s when Hannah knew she really liked him: Liam can make the most out of any situation, and BONUS: he’s an amazing chef!

Later that night, attempting to impress Liam, Hannah dug out some leftover Fourth of July fireworks and lit a full-on mortar in the backyard of her HOA neighborhood. In the middle of the night. In March. The phone started ringing with neighbors asking if it was them, or if they should call the cops because it sounded like a gunshot. Of course they assumed it was the Lyons doing something crazy.

We’re not sure if Hannah impressed Liam the same way he impressed her with his cooking, but he’s still here years later, so it looks like it worked. :)

The question

The proposal

Liam planned to propose in the snowy mountains of Park City, Utah. Unfortunately, on the very first day of vacation, he exploded his knee on a double black diamond, coming to a sudden stop only by wrapping his leg around a tree. The damage: a fully torn ACL, a partially torn meniscus, a sprained MCL, a fractured tibia, and something going on with his kneecap. (He’s fully recovered now; we just want everyone to understand the gravity of the situation!)

Liam was not deterred. With help from Hannah’s cousins and her mom and dad, he made sure she’d be in the right place at the right time. On March 5, 2026, he waited for the snow to fall, then walked her out to the bridge they crossed every morning to get to the slopes.

There, Liam got down on one knee (even with his exploded knee!) and asked Hannah, “Will you marry me?”

She said yes!
