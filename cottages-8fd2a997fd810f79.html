For family & the wedding party

Stay at the Mill

The Mill at Fine Creek has only a dozen guest cottages, and our dream is to fill every one of them with the people we love, so make sure to book before February 15, 2027!

How to book

Reserve a cottage

The cottages will show as unavailable for our weekend on the venue's website. That's on purpose: the Mill is holding them just for our guests until February 15, 2027. After that, any cottages that aren't booked go to people on the Mill's wait list, so please don't wait!

  1. Email Rosalie, the innkeeper, at rosalie@themillatfinecreek.com. You can also call her at (804) 372-9810 (the front desk is closed Mondays and Tuesdays).
  2. Ask for a cottage for Friday, March 17 and/or Saturday, March 18, 2028, and tell her you're with the Wilson / Lyons wedding
    That gets you the 15% wedding discount.
  3. Pay the 50% deposit to confirm. It's non-refundable. The discount comes off the balance you pay at check-in.
  4. Do it all before February 15, 2027, when the Mill's hold on the cottages ends.

Think about staying both nights. We’re having a casual welcome party at Fine Creek Brewing, right next door to the Mill, on Friday, March 17 from 5:30 to 8 pm, so booking Friday and Saturday could be the easiest way to enjoy the whole weekend.

Only want one night? That's fine too. Book the night you need, and if no one from the other wedding books your cottage for the other night, you can ask Rosalie to add it later.

Good to know

Staying at the Mill

  • Check-in at 2 pm, so there's time to settle in before the ceremony. Check-out is 10 am the next morning.
  • Register at Fine Creek Provisions, the general store right next to the cottages.
  • Breakfast is included: pastries, coffee, juice, fruit, yogurt, and granola each morning in the general store.
  • Every cottage has a private sitting room in addition to the bedrooms. The beds are custom-made, with lovely linens.
Questions?

Contact Rosalie, the innkeeper, at rosalie@themillatfinecreek.com or (804) 372-9810. The front desk is closed Mondays and Tuesdays.
