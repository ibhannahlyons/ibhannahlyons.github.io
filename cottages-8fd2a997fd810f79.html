Stay at the Mill

You're on this page because you're one of our people. The Mill at Fine Creek has only a dozen guest cottages, right across the street from where we'll say our vows, and we wanted you to hear about them before anyone else.

Spend the evening with us, walk back to a cozy bed, and wake up to breakfast without having to drive anywhere.

Our dream is to fill every one of them with the people we love, so we're giving you first pick. Please keep this page between us until February 18!

How to book

Reserve a cottage

The cottages will show as unavailable for our date on the venue's website. That's on purpose, because the Mill holds them for our wedding until a year before. To book, you'll need to call.

  1. Call Rosalie, the innkeeper, at (804) 372-9810. The front desk is closed Mondays and Tuesdays.
  2. Ask for a cottage for Saturday, March 18, 2028, and tell her you're with the Lyons / Wilson wedding
    That gets you the 15% wedding discount.
  3. Pay the 50% deposit to confirm. It's non-refundable. The discount comes off the balance you pay at check-in.

Think about staying both nights. We may host the rehearsal dinner at the Mill on Friday, March 17, so booking Friday and Saturday could be the easiest way to enjoy the whole weekend.

Good to know

Staying at the Mill

  • Check-in at 2 pm, so there's time to settle in before the ceremony. Check-out is 10 am the next morning.
  • Register at Fine Creek Provisions, the general store right next to the cottages.
  • Breakfast is included: pastries, coffee, juice, fruit, yogurt, and granola each morning in the general store.
  • Every cottage has a private sitting room in addition to the bedrooms. The beds are custom-made, with lovely linens.
Questions?

Contact Rosalie, the innkeeper, at cottages@themillatfinecreek.com or (804) 372-9810. The front desk is closed Mondays and Tuesdays.
